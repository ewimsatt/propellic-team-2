Hero has no destination headline
The hero image is beautiful but contains no text overlay stating the destination, property name, or value proposition. Visitors have to scroll to understand what they're looking at.
Recommended Fix Add a prominent H1 overlay on the hero image — e.g. "Book direct & save 15% — Santorini's #1 boutique hotel." Keep contrast ratio above 4.5:1.
Warning

On a 375px viewport, the primary booking button requires scrolling ~180px. Users who arrive via paid search may bounce before ever seeing it.
Recommended Fix Add a sticky bottom bar on mobile containing the CTA and a starting price anchor ("From $149/night · Book Now").

Trust & Reassurance
Reviews, credentials & cancellation policy visibility
1 Critical 1 Warning 1 Good
Critical
No trust badges near the booking widget
TripAdvisor rating, Google review count, and security seals are absent from the booking area. Trust signals adjacent to CTAs can lift conversions by 10–25%.
Recommended Fix Add a trust bar directly above or below the booking widget: TripAdvisor traveler rating, Google star count, and a "Secure checkout" badge. Limit to 3 logos to avoid clutter.
Warning
Cancellation policy is only in the footer
Travelers are highly sensitive to cancellation risk. Burying the policy in footer text creates uncertainty that causes hesitation at the checkout step.
Recommended Fix Surface a one-line policy summary directly beside the CTA — e.g., "Free cancellation up to 48 hours before arrival" — with a link to full terms.
Good
SSL certificate is valid and visible
The site loads over HTTPS with a valid certificate and the browser padlock is displayed. This is table stakes but eliminates a common drop-off trigger.
Recommended Fix No action needed. Ensure your booking iframe or third-party widget also runs on HTTPS to avoid mixed-content warnings.
Form & Booking Flow
Date picker, guest selector & checkout friction
2 Warnings 1 Good
Warning
Date picker requires 3 clicks before showing availability
Users must click a field, select check-in, then select check-out before any availability or pricing appears. Each additional interaction step has a measurable drop-off rate.
Recommended Fix Use an inline date-range picker that renders on page load with the current week pre-selected. Show a live price update as soon as dates are chosen.
Warning
Guest selector defaults to 1 adult
Most leisure travel bookings are for couples or families. Defaulting to 1 adult creates a mismatch and adds a required interaction before the user can see relevant pricing.
Recommended Fix Default to 2 adults. Consider adding a "Traveling with kids?" prompt as a secondary option to guide family bookings into the right room category.
Good
Price is shown before the final confirmation step
The total price including taxes is visible on the booking summary page before the user enters payment details. This reduces sticker-shock abandonment at checkout.
Recommended Fix No action needed. Consider also surfacing a per-night breakdown to reinforce value perception alongside the total.
